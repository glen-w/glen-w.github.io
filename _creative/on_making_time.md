Leave me. Alone!  
i’m sick of my phone.  
But also — stay.  
please.

We’ve got puzzles to do  
and your silly grin keeps me sane,  
or at least  
somewhere  
in the vicinity of the ballpark.

i’ve lost count of the names  
on the listes de choses à faire  
life’s not fair  
les coeurs en vrac sur les lignes —  
a bittersweet affair  
of love  
and air.

you laugh  
i freeze  
you glitch

We solve!  
and unsolve,  
resolve to resolve the unresolved  
dissolve,  
reconnect  
and solve again

crossed words adventuring on all axes  
letters scattered in the static  
one missed connection away  
from a perfect present tense.

i say something half-wise,  
eyes half open  
hearts open wide.

i’ve been buffering for thirty minutes,  
at the most inopportune of times.

bona nit,  
you beautiful nerd —  
We must trust  
that our moment  
will arrive.
