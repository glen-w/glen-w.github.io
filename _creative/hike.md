We climbed to the top of an unfamiliar hill  
with a flag and a guest book  
and a soft déjà vu.

In the expansive view  
i was all but forgotten,  
but the parched earth  
somehow remembered you.

Each step seemed to delete  
a thought  
i didn’t know  
i was ready to lose.

At times you walked a few steps ahead,  
i looked back  
and found that no world followed.  
Or you were a few steps behind,  
not trying to hide your fear of falling,

and with your footsteps near,  
the path forward  
was mine to clear.
