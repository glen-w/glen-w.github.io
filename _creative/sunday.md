it was supposed to be a day of rest,  
but we awoke early.  
passed the first test.  
we got in the car,  
arrived with plenty of time.  
i didn’t want to leave,  
but there were no tears.  
we weren’t really sad,  
just happy to have been  
here  
together.

the machine scanned all my gear,  
i half slept,  
then swept away the mess on my desktop.  
now the laundry’s drying  
amidst terracotta shards  
strewn by the wind.

a siren sounds,  
a reminder that our species still tries to care.

i already miss your silly smile.

i’m glad i’m here,  
i wish i was there.
