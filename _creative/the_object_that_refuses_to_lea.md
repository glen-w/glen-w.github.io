Wait!  
i’ve managed to hold on this long  
so why waste me now?  
i was procured years ago  
for dying hair or repairing the broken  
shards of  
something  
that fell from grace  
or for manipulating  
something  
infected on your face.

why now?  
why not just hold on forever  
to the past

keep me here  
as a reminder  
of all you lost, and  
all that’s left to gain.

Move me from here to there  
again and again  
hold on forever,  
i’m just… stuff!

No matter,  
i take up no space,  
keep me here,  
just in case,  
just in case

Why not hold on forever  
to the past  
just in case.
