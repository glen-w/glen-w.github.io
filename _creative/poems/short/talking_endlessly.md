There’s a Palestine protest  
They’ve signed the ocean treaty  
And the bureaucrats gatekeep burgers  
We live in the dumbest of timelines  
and i’ve no excuse for my silence

They will say i was complicit  
and i won’t argue;  
i was just so tired  
from talking  
endlessly  
about the meta feedback on the annotated outline  
of the core pillar strategy.
