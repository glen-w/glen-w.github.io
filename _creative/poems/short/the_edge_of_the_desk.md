There’s a fine line  
Between this and that  
Here and there and now and then and  
never  
again and and again and again

There’s a fine line  
of dust  
on the desk,  
of trust

your hand  
loving  
gentle  
around my neck  
of memories  
they only just left.

There’s a fine line  
Between the implored  
and the ignored  
and the  
“what the hell is this all for.”

A persistent ghost from the past  
haunts my dreams,  
even though you did,  
finally?  
maybe?  
pick yourself up off the floor.

There’s a fine line,  
between love,  
and love a little too late.
