Folk clubs, sessions, dances, and festivals in the UK are easy to miss if you do not already know who to ask. Listings live on posters, Facebook groups, and club sites that do not talk to each other.

Folk Directory is one place to look them up. The live site is the directory.

I add and correct listings as I find them. The work is keeping it true, not building another brochure.
