Ocean timelines were mostly heavy. A small account that only posted a friendly emoji seascape every couple of hours was the opposite kind of noise.

[@tiny_seascape](https://x.com/tiny_seascape) is that bot: display name “tiny seascape,” bio “a little splash of the big blue, every couple of hours,” pointing at [@LittleBlueLette](https://x.com/LittleBlueLette) and LittleBlueLetter.com. It joined in November 2019. Little Blue Letter described it as a friendly Twitter bot that posts random emoji seascapes every few hours.

Archived here as a finished side project from the Little Blue Letter years.
