A lot of ocean news is heavy. There were younger people doing ocean work whose stories did not have a friendly place to land.

Little Blue Letter was a short newsletter of uplifting ocean stuff, curated by young ocean leaders.

I spoke about it at CommOCEAN in Southampton in December 2018: [Engaging audiences with a fun and friendly newsletter: The Little Blue Letter Story](/library/glen_wright_engaging-audiences-fun-friendly-newsletter-little-blue-letter-story/). I am not running it as a live list from here.
