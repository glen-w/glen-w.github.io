Ocean talks stacked up faster than anyone could keep a personal list. Filters for organiser, topic, and language helped; a shared sheet was clearer than another newsletter inbox.

Ocean webinars is that sheet: a collaborative listing of more than two hundred ocean webinars (events from about 2019 through 2023), branded with Little Blue Letter, with columns for link, title, organiser(s), topic(s), language, and date. People could browse what was already there or add a webinar through a form.

It was promoted in Little Blue Letter during the 2020 webinar boom. The sheet still opens; the listing is archived here as finished work, not an active calendar.
