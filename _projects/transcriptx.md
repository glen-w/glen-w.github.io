A transcript is usually where the work stops: you have the words, and then either a cloud product interprets them for you, or you are left with a file. I want the analysis, and I want it to stay here.

TranscriptX is for that middle. You bring files from Whisper, Otter, or a manual export. It does not transcribe audio. It runs analysis on your machine so you can see speakers, tension, themes, and how a conversation changes when the same people meet again.

The web app is the usual way in. You can also script a run. Outputs are kept so you can go back to them. Groups of transcripts are possible, still experimental.

It is in beta. I want it to become a companion for personal recordings and voice notes — still local, still something I can sit with rather than a verdict.
