**Academia Obscura** is a playful excavation of the stranger, sillier, and often unintentionally hilarious corners of academic life.

Born as a long-running blog and later a book, it collects curious research, baffling studies, and the moments where higher education collapses under its own weight. It is a love letter to curiosity taken too far: knowledge production is often weird, occasionally wonderful, and never quite as tidy as it pretends to be.

The dedicated site is at **[academiaobscura.com](https://academiaobscura.com/)**.

## The Book

*Academia Obscura: The Hidden Silly Side of Higher Education* was published by Unbound (2018).
