**Sample topics covered:**
- Law of the Sea and UNCLOS at 40  
- Conservation in Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction (ABNJ)  
- Regional Ocean Governance (e.g. UN Regional Seas Programme)  
- Deep Sea Mining and Monitoring, Control & Surveillance  
- Ocean & Climate Linkages, Marine Spatial Planning  
- International Negotiation Simulations (BBNJ, GBF)  
- Guest speakers from Tara, UNESCO, Client Earth
**Format:** Weekly sessions, student presentations, invited experts, policy brief assignments.
