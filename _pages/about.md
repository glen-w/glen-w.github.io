Hi, I’m Glen — a knowledge wrangler and data translator working at the heart of the renewable energy transition. At REN21, I’m part of the Knowledge & Data team, where I help make sense of complex energy trends, shape the Global Status Report, and support a global community of contributors working toward a just, data-informed shift to renewables.

In a past life, I was immersed in international ocean governance — working on high seas biodiversity, regional cooperation, and marine policy at IDDRI, and representing civil society in global treaty negotiations. These days, I still teach ocean governance at Sciences Po, and continue to follow developments in the marine space with interest (and the occasional opinionated tweet).

I’m passionate about open data, accessible knowledge, and turning tangled technical issues into insights people can act on. I also believe a good chart and a better metaphor can go a long way.
