# Ambient Findability

This page is about ambient findability - the ability to discover information and resources in your environment without actively searching for them.

## What is Ambient Findability?

Ambient findability refers to the ability to locate information, people, and resources in your immediate environment through passive discovery rather than active search. It's about making relevant information discoverable through context, proximity, and serendipity.

## Key Concepts

- **Passive Discovery**: Information finds you rather than you finding it
- **Context Awareness**: Information is presented based on your current situation
- **Proximity**: Relevant resources are highlighted based on location or relationship
- **Serendipity**: Unexpected but valuable discoveries through environmental cues

## Applications

- **Smart Cities**: Street signs that adapt to your needs
- **Digital Workspaces**: Tools that surface relevant information
- **Learning Environments**: Educational resources that appear when needed
- **Social Networks**: Connections that emerge through shared contexts

## Future Directions

As technology becomes more embedded in our environments, ambient findability will play an increasingly important role in how we discover and interact with information.
