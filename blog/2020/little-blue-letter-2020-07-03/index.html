3 July 2020 — Finding the Deep - Curator’s Story

Created on July 03, 2020

2020   ·   LittleBlueLetter   ·   Little Blue Letter

Sent 3 July 2020. Part of Little Blue Letter.

“There's probably more history now preserved underwater than in all the museums of the world combined."
~ Dr. Robert Ballard
Ocean update
One-fifth of the seafloor has now been mapped! 

To celebrate World Hydrography Day, the Nippon Foundation-GEBCO Seabed 2030 Project announced that 19% of the world’s seafloor has now been mapped to modern standards. Seabed 2030 is an ambitious initiative to map 100% of the ocean floor by 2030 - very important for exploration and scientific advancement. This is an impressive feat as in 2017 only 6% of the seabed was mapped!

Finding the Deep - Curator’s Story
  • I’ve known I wanted to be a deep sea scientist since I was 18-years-old. The quote for this week’s newsletter is by Dr. Robert Ballard, whose TED Talk on the importance of ocean exploration inspired me to become a marine scientist.
  • I was lucky enough to get to meet and sail with Dr. Ballard on the Exploration Vessel the E/V Nautilus and sat next to him while we explored the Eastern Pacific with the remotely operated vehicle, Hercules. 

  • ​The deep sea is waiting to be discovered, and while onboard we found and sampled what is likely a new species of velutinid snail!

A new species? (Ocean Exploration Trust)

  • For any aspiring ocean explorers out there, my advice is to follow your passion with perseverance and determination. I’m incredibly lucky to get to discover more about the deep sea every week, and I hope more students and researchers will be able to as well!
