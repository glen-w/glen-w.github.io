21 October 2017 — I'm not joking

Created on October 21, 2017

2017   ·   LittleBlueLetter   ·   Little Blue Letter

Sent 21 October 2017. Part of Little Blue Letter.

"How inappropriate to call this planet Earth when it is clearly Ocean."
~ Arthur C. Clarke
The climate conference in Bonn wraps up today. Hosts Fiji launched their Ocean Pathway initiative yesterday, building on the Call for Action from the UN Ocean Conference. The Pathway "flags the possibility of a UNFCCC agenda item and a work programme by 2019 and ensures the insertion of the ocean into NDCs" (Nationally Determined Contributions - the climate commitments made by States).

The International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT) is also meeting this week, and many States want to increase the quota of bluefin tuna, which is slowly starting to show signs of recovery. Here's a handy FAQ. (NB: campaigners call ICCAT the 'International Cartel to Catch All Tuna', but you didn't hear that from me!)

Open Communications for the Ocean (OCTO) just launched a new, free repository of marine science - MarXiv (rhymes with archive) - which aims to "give managers and planners free access to the science they need, which is usually otherwise hidden behind expensive journal fees". Consider posting pre-prints of your papers!

Long-nosed chimaera
In case you missed it
UNESCO's International Oceanographic Commission (IOC) recently released the Global Ocean Science Report, which assesses the status and trends in ocean science capacity around the world. The report offers a global record of who, how, and where ocean science is conducted.

Check out this map of commitments made at the UN Oceans Conference in June. 

World Ocean Radio has a nice set of audio essays on the future of the Arctic, looking at governance, the Arctic Council, climate change, and the health and welfare of Indigenous people.
Scientists have found a 227-year old Greenland whale shark, the oldest (known) living invertebrate.
When this shark was a baby, the US was still a British colony!

Academia Obscura: The Hidden Silly Side of Higher Education was released this week (penned by yours truly). Meanwhile, the Flat Earth International Conference took place in Raleigh, North Carolina. Honestly, I'm not joking.
