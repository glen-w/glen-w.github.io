Island life 
Isle de Jean Charles in Louisiana has already lost 98% of its land to erosion, subsidence, and a rising sea. In 2016, a $48-million subsidy was given to relocate residents, effectively making them the first government-recognized climate refugees in the US. Now, a virtual reality project is aiming to preserve the Biloxi-Chitimacha-Choctaw people’s culture too. My visit with Billiot is a unique VR archive of the area's rich oral history - take a look here.
The gravel that holds up the only road connecting the Isle with the mainland has slowly eroded, leaving a precarious one-lane track.
From the darkness...
Elsewhere, new technology is lighting up dark fishing fleets. Illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing (IUU) has a huge impact on marine life and constitutes an estimated 11-26 million tonnes (12-28%) of fishing world-wide. Monitoring boats fishing at night has been one of many challenges, but last week Global Fishing Watch revealed a new imaging system capable of detecting vessels that fish at night.
Beyond Blue
A new ocean exploration video game was unveiled last week at the E3 gaming convention. In Beyond Blue, the player takes the role of Mirai, a research team leader that sets out on an expedition to see the ocean in a more meaningful way. Look out for its release in early 2019. In the meantime, check out the trailer.
