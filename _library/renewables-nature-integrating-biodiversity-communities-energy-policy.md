abstract: "How can renewable energy accelerate the energy transition while also delivering\
  \ tangible benefits for nature and communities? \n\nThis joint online event hosted\
  \ by The Nature Conservancy (TNC) and REN21 will explore this question through the\
  \ lens of the newly adopted Net-Zero Industry Act (NZIA) and its provisions on non-price\
  \ criteria (NPCs) in renewable energy auctions. Bringing together perspectives from\
  \ policymakers, industry leaders, and civil society, the event will highlight how\
  \ well-crafted energy policy can drive climate action while also restoring ecosystems\
  \ and empowering local communities. We’ll take a deep dive into the role of NPCs\
  \ within the EU’s new policy framework and examine their current and future implementation\
  \ across EU Member States."
