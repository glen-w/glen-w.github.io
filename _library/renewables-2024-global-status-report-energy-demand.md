abstract: 'Despite a notable decline in the prices of fossil fuels and other energy
  commodities in the first half of 2023, wholesale electricity prices remained high
  in many countries, negatively affecting energy-consuming sectors.


  Inflation and high interest rates continued to hamper investment, particularly in
  emerging markets that face debt burdens and a higher cost of capital.


  Meanwhile, massive subsidies to fossil fuels remain prevalent and have distorted
  the market, placing renewables at a disadvantage.


  Renewables nonetheless continued to prove attractive as an affordable and secure
  energy source in 2023, with investments rising to a record USD 623 billion and corporate
  power purchase agreements reaching a record 46 gigawatts (GW)'
