abstract: The toolkit provides an overview of 28 economic, de-risking and financing
  instruments that can be used for financing industry decarbonisation, both in emerging
  markets and developing economies (EMDEs) and in developed countries. The description
  of instruments is complemented with a suite of case studies to showcase how they
  are being implemented. In addition, the toolkit highlights a preliminary economic
  assessment for a selection of low-carbon technologies in various hard-to-abate industries
  in different country settings, which could benefit from some instruments in the
  toolkit.
