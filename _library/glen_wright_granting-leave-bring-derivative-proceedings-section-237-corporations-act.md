  tried under section 237(2)(d).The Judge held that claims that the companies in question
  had made loans to directors, "alienated" share options to directors' companies,
  provided excessive remuneration, diverted business to another company and engaged
  in oppressive conduct each satisfied the test in section 237(2)(d).
