  International Relations (IDDRI) in Paris, as the first guest editor of Wiley’s Exchanges
  blog. For one week, starting May 11, 2015, he will take over the blog’s reins to
  explore “The PhD Path Less Travelled.” Mr. Wright, who is also a PhD candidate at
  the Australian National University and the editor of Academia Obscura, was selected
  from a pool of entrants in the first Early Career Researcher (ECR) Takeover Week
  contest held by Wiley Advisors-Wiley’s free membership program designed to connect
  with and learn from early career researchers. The Advisor program gives new researchers
