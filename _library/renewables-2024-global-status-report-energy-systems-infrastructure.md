  \ blocks of the energy system, as well as technology advancements that are enabling\
  \ the integration of higher shares of variable renewable electricity.\n\nGlobal\
  \ electricity demand increased 27% between 2013 and 2023, putting pressure on existing\
  \ power grids. In many locations, distributed generation is growing faster than\
  \ grid capacity. \n\nUtility-scale battery storage grew 120% in 2023. \n\nPumped\
  \ storage remains the most common solution for large-scale utility storage.\n\n\
  Investment in hydrogen increased 203% in 2023 to reach USD 10.4 billion. The bulk\
  \ of hydrogen production worldwide continues to come from fossil fuel sources."
