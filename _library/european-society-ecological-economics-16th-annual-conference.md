abstract: 'The title of the conference is “Looking for (the) Just Judges – Promoting
  Sustainable and Inclusive Well-Being in Turbulent Times” referencing to the missing
  panel in the alterpiece of the Saint Bavo cathedral in Ghent.

  The conference brings together ecological economists from across Europe, and is
  organized in collaboration with the city of Ghent and several local NGOs. It will
  host a series of keynote lectures, parallel sessions and poster sessions. Participants
  also had the opportunity to submit Open Special Subtracks (OSS) and Closed Special
  Sessions (CSS) to complement the conference programme.'
