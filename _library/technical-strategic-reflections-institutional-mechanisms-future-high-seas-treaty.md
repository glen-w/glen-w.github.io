abstract: The negotiations for the development of a legally binding instrument on
  high seas biodiversity began at the end of 2017 and are currently suspended due
  to the public health crisis. The negotiations have focused mainly on the substantive
  issues at stake, but negotiators must also carefully consider the development of
  the treaty's institutional mechanisms, which will be crucial for ensuring effectiveness.
