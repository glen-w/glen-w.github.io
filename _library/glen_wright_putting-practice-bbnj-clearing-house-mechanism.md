abstract: 'The much anticipated first meeting of the Conference of the Parties (CoP1)
  is happening on 11-22 January 2027, one year after the BBNJ Agreement has entered
  into force and became an international law. Prior to this, the BBNJ Preparatory
  Commission held three two-week meetings from 2025 to 2026, where delegations have
  negotiated and generally agreed on the preparations for CoP1, including the institutional
  set-up of BBNJ, which will be the backbone of its implementation.

  The challenge is clear – how to translate the BBNJ provisions to actionable points
  to ensure effective, transparent, and inclusive implementation of the Agreement.
  In preparation for CoP1, the Ministry of Fisheries and Ocean Resources of Maldives
  and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Thailand, in collaboration with the High
  Seas Alliance and International Union for Conservation of Nature Asia Regional Office,
  are convening a two-day retreat to facilitate knowledge exchange and a capacity-building
  session on BBNJ institutional issues and key aspects of implementation.

  The retreat, which will be held at the margins of the Asia-Pacific Ocean Week in
  Bangkok on 26-30 October 2026, will also provide a one-day participation for delegates
  to learn from relevant sessions. Objectives The regional retreat aims to convene
  Asia government delegates for a learning exchange in preparation for CoP1. It aims
  to facilitate constructive discussions among countries and share informed views
  by the experts.'
