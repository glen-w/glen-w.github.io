I design and facilitate workshops, retreats, and learning journeys for teams working on oceans, energy, and climate. This includes strategy and reflection sessions, technical deep dives (e.g. ocean governance, energy transitions), and capacity-building workshops on topics such as data literacy, policy processes, and effective collaboration. My approach is structured but participatory, with a focus on psychological safety, clear outcomes, and practical next steps rather than abstract discussion.
