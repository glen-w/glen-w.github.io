I offer one-to-one coaching for students, early-career professionals, and mid-career practitioners working in (or around) oceans, energy, climate, and adjacent fields. Typical topics include career transitions, moving between academia and policy, navigating international processes, and finding more sustainable ways of working. Sessions can be one-off consultations or part of a longer-term coaching relationship, and are particularly welcoming to people who feel out of place in conventional institutional environments.
