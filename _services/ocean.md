## A clear read of the ocean governance room

I help NGOs, alliances, funders and research groups work through international ocean governance: the high seas and the BBNJ Agreement on biodiversity beyond national jurisdiction, including environmental impact assessment under the Agreement, marine spatial planning, and the work of regional fisheries management organisations (RFMOs). I have spent more than a decade in and around the UN process that produced the High Seas Treaty.

**What you get**

- A policy brief for a named decision or meeting
- Support before and during negotiations
- A review of positions or submissions
- A process tracker
- A stakeholder map

Offshore wind, wave and tidal energy sit here too, as ocean-governance questions about space, rules and who decides.

**A good fit if** you need someone who knows the process well enough to tell you what is realistic, and what to do about it.
