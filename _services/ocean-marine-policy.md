I provide strategic advice on international ocean governance, with a particular focus on the high seas, seabed mining, and regional cooperation. Drawing on over a decade of experience with the UN High Seas Treaty process, I help organisations, alliances, and institutions understand treaty implications, navigate regional and global frameworks, and sharpen their policy and advocacy strategies. This can include tailored briefings, negotiation support, and review of positions, reports, and submissions.
