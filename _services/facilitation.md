## Sessions that leave your team clearer about what to do next

I design and run workshops for teams working on oceans, energy and climate. I teach [_Marine Policy & Ocean Governance_](/projects/marine-policy-ocean-governance/) at Sciences Po.

**Formats**

- Strategy and reflection sessions
- Technical deep dives on a policy process or topic
- Capacity-building workshops
- Orientation sessions for a group that needs to get its bearings before deciding what to do next

**How I work**

Sessions are participatory and well paced. Each one starts from outcomes we agree in advance, gives everyone room to contribute, and ends with practical next steps.

**What you get**

- An agreed agenda and outcomes up front
- A short workshop report with decisions and next steps

Booked at a day rate, for a single workshop or a short series.

Not sure a full workshop is what you need? A 30-minute call is enough to work out the right format.
