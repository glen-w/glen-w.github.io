I support organisations working on renewable energy and energy systems to interpret global trends and apply them to their specific context. Building on my experience with the Renewables Global Status Report, I help teams frame evidence-based narratives, design indicators, and turn complex datasets into clear insights for decision-makers. Services range from strategic input on energy-transition policies to data-driven storyline development for reports, funding proposals, and communications.
