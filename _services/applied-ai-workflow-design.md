I help NGOs, research groups, and small teams make practical, ethical use of AI and lightweight digital tools in their everyday work. This can involve mapping existing workflows, identifying sensible use cases (e.g. drafting, summarising, data handling), and setting up simple, robust processes that reduce friction without over-engineering. I also offer plain-language introductions to AI concepts, risks, and guardrails, tailored to organisations that want to experiment thoughtfully rather than chase hype.
