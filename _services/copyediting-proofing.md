I provide copyediting and proofing services for reports, academic articles, policy briefs, funding proposals, and other written outputs. My focus is on clarity, coherence, and accessibility: preserving technical accuracy while making texts easier to read for their intended audiences. I can work at different levels of intervention, from light proofing to more substantive editing and restructuring, and I am comfortable working with both individual authors and larger project teams.
