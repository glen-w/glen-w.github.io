### Qualifications

I graduated from the University of Nottingham with upper second-class honours in Law (with American Law) (LL.B). As part of this degree, I completed a one year exchange at the University of Texas.

I am currently studying for a Masters of Environmental Law at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), where I have attained a distinction average thus far.

Understanding of Policy Development Processes and Environmental Legal Issues
I have recently developed my understanding of policy development processes by working as a Paralegal in the Energy and Resources group at Freehills, where my work often involves writing memorandums regarding current environmental and mining law policy developments. I also work as a Research Assistant at the Initiative on Climate Change Law and Policy at UNSW, which similarly involves researching and summarising policy developments.

Furthermore, I am currently working on two projects relating to environmental policy development. The first is a paper discussing the readiness of Australia’s transmission network to connect large-scale renewable energy generators. This paper traces the development of policy from inception to publication by the federal government. The second is a submission to a public consultation in the UK regarding the structure of a proposed feed-in tariff policy.

These experiences have helped me to understand how policy is made, including how public consultations are conducted, how consultation submissions are written and how policy agencies respond to these submissions.

As a Paralegal and Research Assistant I have not only learned about policy processes, but also acquired in-depth knowledge of areas of many areas of environmental law, particularly in NSW, but also nationally and internationally. At university I have studied Comparative Environmental Law, International Climate Change Law and International Wildlife Law. Next semester I will be studying Renewable Energy Law and Australian Environmental Law. I have written a thesis about climate change law, which has been published in a peer-reviewed journal and I recently won the EDO and Maddocks student essay writing competition with my essay on the regulation of the voluntary carbon market.

POLICY LAWYER

### Environmental Defenders Office Ltd

The EDO is a community legal centre specialising in public interest advocacy regarding environmental law and policy. We are seeking a Policy Lawyer to be part of a highly active and successful legal policy team. The Policy Lawyer will research and write submissions on high profile government proposals, develop discussion papers and other active policy initiatives, play a key stakeholder role in issues relating to environmental law and policy and provide legal policy advice to environmental groups. Salary $40,266 to $64,878 (depending on experience) plus superannuation.

An undergraduate degree in law

Commitment to the protection of the environment and public interest environmental law Duty Statement
The duties of the Policy Lawyer are:
1)  Policy and law reform responding to State and federal government policy and legislative proposals on important public interest environmental and planning law issues preparing written submissions and proactive discussion papers on topical issues, and appearing at parliamentary inquiries preparing drafting instructions for legislative proposals and amendments developing policy positions on specific public interest environmental and planning law issues undertaking legal consultancy work on behalf of conservation groups liaising, and attending meetings, with relevant stakeholders, including ELO meetings

4)  Administration assisting with office administration, such as library, computer and precedent systems maintaining appropriate records word-processing of own work
5) General
