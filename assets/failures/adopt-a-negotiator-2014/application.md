What roles does your country play in the UNFCCC? *
We'd like to learn more about your understanding of the roles your country plays in the climate fight internationally. Does it negotiate as part of a bloc? Is it a leader? a spoiler? complicated? (100 words or less)

The UK is part of the EU, a leader in the UNFCCC. The EU: endorses the 2° limit; has a -20%/2020 emissions target; and supports a post-2020 agreement. EU leadership has faltered during the economic crisis, e.g. weakened renewables targets.

The UK advocates a stronger -30% emissions target. PM ████ Cameron heads the “greenest government ever”, investing financial and diplomatic resources into advancing climate action through the UNFCCC. The UK also engages the G8 and G20.

Do you have other relevant experience with international or environmental negotiations? *

Presented at a number of conferences, as well as attending Rio+20. Peer-reviewed papers on: climate mitigation/policy; REDD+; IRENA. Courses: International Climate Law; Climate Change Law & Policy; Environmental Markets: Renewable Energy Law; and various specialist environmental law courses.

Tell us what national solutions to climate change you think are most important right now. * Is your country working on a climate mitigation or adaptation plan? Removing fossil fuel subsidies? Clean energy access? Let us know what actions your country most needs to take. (200 words or less)

The UK already has a Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act (2006), promoting microgeneration, and the Climate Change Act (2008), setting an -80%/2050 emissions target. The Government has produced a Risk Assessment and Adaptation Programme, and implemented a range of climate measures.

The UK needs to augment and maintain clean energy support as fossil fuels still dominate the energy mix. The UK needs to move from planning to action regarding adaptation: recent extreme weather events brought this into sharp relief (drought in early 2012, followed by flooding in winter 2012).

What are the climate-related issues that connect with people in your country? * Tell us about what people are concerned about in your country? Is it health? Food security? Conservation? perhaps something else? What issues pique public interest. (200 words or less)

High energy prices and reliance on imports has made renewables a hot topic. Recent extreme weather events have people talking about adaptation, as the impacts of climate change are increasingly real.

The economic crisis, unemployment and cost of living remain major concerns. This provides an opportunity, not yet fully utilised, to connect climate with economic issues, e.g. through discussion of the ‘Green Economy’.
