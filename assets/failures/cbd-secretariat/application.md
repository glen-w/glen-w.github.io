### Org. Setting and Reporting

The United Nations Environment Programme is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. At the 1992 Earth Summit in Rio de Janeiro, world leaders agreed on a comprehensive strategy for 'sustainable development'. One of the key agreements adopted at Rio was the Convention on Biological Diversity. The Convention has three main objectives: the conservation of biological diversity, the sustainable use of its components, and the fair and equitable sharing of the benefits arising from the use of genetic resources. The Secretariat of the Convention on Biological Diversity (SCBD) is located in Montreal and is administered by United Nations Environment Programme. This post is located in the Convention on Biological Diversity, Science, Society and Sustainable Futures Division. Under the general guidance of the Head of Division and the direct supervision of the Head of the Biodiversity Science, Policy and Governance Unit, the incumbent will perform the following duties:

### Responsibilities

A. Facilitates the preparation of neutral scientific, technical and information documents and the provision of expert advice on the conservation and sustainable use of marine and coastal biodiversity, including the Aichi Biodiversity Targets and Sustainable Development Goals, to COP and its subsidiary bodies as well as other scientific and technical preparatory processes.
B. Facilitates the execution of the various components of the Convention’s work on marine and coastal biodiversity.
C. Provides scientific and technical support to Parties and other stakeholders in the integration of marine and coastal biodiversity into relevant international/regional processes.
D. Supports the implementation of capacity development activities among Parties and other stakeholders on marine and coastal biodiversity, including through the Sustainable Ocean Initiative. E. Oversees the preparation and dissemination of communication products and awarenessbuilding information on marine and coastal biodiversity.
F. Provides scientific and technical support to the monitoring of the status and trends of marine and coastal biodiversity, and review and evaluation of the Aichi Biodiversity Targets and Sustainable Development Goals, and the development of the post2020 biodiversity framework. G Performs any other duties as assigned by the Executive Secretary and the Heads of the Science, Society and Sustainable Futures Division and the Biodiversity Science, Policy and Government Unit.

### Education

Advanced university degree (Master’s Degree or equivalent) in marine biology, marine environmental sciences, coastal management, oceanography or other marine related subjects is required. A first level university degree in combination with two additional years of qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

### Work Experience
