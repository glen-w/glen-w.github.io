Product Manager - Marine Reserve Program | June 2020 | 1 of 2

To whom it may concern,

As an ambitious researcher and passionate advocate with extensive experience in marine governance and a longstanding interest in technology, I am excited to apply for the position of Product Manager - Marine Reserve Program at Global Fishing Watch. In my role as Senior Researcher in International Marine Policy at the Institute for Sustainable Development and International Relations (IDDRI) , I am engaged in a range of activities that aim to facilitate regional cooperation and strengthen ocean governance. I produce policy-relevant research and collaborate with NGOs and scientists to advocate for positive changes to conservation and management of marine biodiversity.

I share GFW’s mission - to protect the ocean through effective governance - and believe that new technological tools can be a transformative force for marine conservation by increasing transparency. I am keen to contribute my knowledge, skills and network to advance this mission.

### Knowledge and experience

As part of the STRONG High Seas project , I have developed a work package on monitoring, control and surveillance (MCS), focussing on emerging technologies, international regulations, and advocating for MCS in a future treaty. I organised a fact-ﬁnding mission to expand our network and assess current challenges, meeting with experts from the FAO, academia, NGOs and industry, and visiting the UK Satellite Applications Catapult. This established a foundation for: international expert workshops on MCS technologies and policies ; a regional workshop in South America, which I conducted in Spanish; and a UN side event, co-hosted with GFW . We produced two reports, proposing options for a treaty and providing the basis for ongoing advocacy. Interventions by GFW staff at our events, and lively exchanges on the sidelines, gave me a deeper understanding of GFW’s work and strategy.

### Relationship building

I played a key role in establishing the Partnership for Regional Ocean Governance , working with colleagues to identify strategic partners and collaborating to develop projects that have attracted signiﬁcant long-term support and funding. I coordinate with a wide range of actors to implement these projects - including government, research institutes, and UN and EU bodies - and am responsible for tracking progress and reporting.

I am open and easy-going, and ﬁnd that my genuine interest in people enables me to quickly form personal connections. I follow up with new contacts, sharing publications and information where appropriate - in my experience, the most effective way to turn connections into collaborators is to be friendly and useful. I assiduously update my contact list, noting relevant context that allows me to easily identify potential collaborators and sources of up-to-date information.

These relationships have provided me and my institute with excellent opportunities, including invitations to speak at high-level closed-door workshops, lead development of signiﬁcant publications, and provide input to policy-making processes. I have also relied on these skills to bring success to a range of personal projects, such as building a pool of dedicated readers and curators of the Little Blue Letter and crowdfunding a book published by Penguin Random House .

### Analytical and strategic skills

Working for a small institute, I am required to strategically develop a work programme that can, with limited resources, generate both impact and visibility in a crowded and complex ﬁeld. I continuously monitor current developments, seeking niches where we can contribute useful analysis and identifying speciﬁc roles we can fulﬁll. I have thereby developed a varied body of research and events that has secured our position as both a leader on previously neglected critical issues (ﬁsheries, MCS, impact assessment) and as an objective and respected voice in international policy processes.
