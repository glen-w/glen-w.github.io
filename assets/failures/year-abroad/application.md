## EXAMPLE OF ABILITY TO COPE IN UNFORESEEN CIRCUMSTANCES

The most recent demonstration of my ability to cope occurred upon starting university. I had budgeted and applied for my loan well in advance, however, the student loans company made an administrative error which meant I did not receive my loan until mid-November. When I did receive it, the amount was half what I expected and, due to the error, my bursary from Nottingham was also delayed. As I had budgeted for the year, this presented me with a great problem.

I considered my options and how I might sustain myself while the SLC resolved the issues. I rewrote my budget, allowing myself less money for certain ‘non-essentials’. I spoke to an advisor at my bank to ensure that I would not be charged if I exceeded my overdraft and I consulted my hall who allowed me to pay some of my accommodation fee, paying the balance later. I spoke to the University’s financial support team, both about the bursary and about access to emergency funds if I should need them; knowing that I had ‘backup’ sources of finances if needed gave me peace of mind. I managed to stick to the new budget and kept within my overdraft limit. As I couldn’t recover the deficit from the SLC, I worked extra hours over the holidays in order to make up for it.

## WHY YOU SHOULD BE SELECTED FOR YOUR PREFERRED DEGREE PROGRAM

The University of Texas at Austin consistently ranks amongst the world’s top universities and is widely respected. As a student dedicated to a career at the bar I need to stand out to succeed and studying at UT would help me accomplish this.

I want to attend UT to take advantage of their excellent academic facilities. It has recently undergone refurbishment, offering a state of the art teaching environment. UT offers a number of modules which would add depth and focus to my degree. I am particularly attracted to American Constitutional Law having found Comparative Civil Liberties very enjoyable. I would enjoy taking more unusual modules such capital punishment law. I prefer interactive learning, a teaching method favoured by UT, for instance their use of the Socratic Method, and I feel that this would allow me to excel.

I was a semi-finalist in our first year mooting competition and am currently taking part in this year’s competition. UT has two working court rooms which provide a true to life mooting experience. Attorneys act as judges for internal competitions and train the team for interscholastic moots. I savour the prospect of improving my advocacy skills.

UT runs an on-the-job training program. In particular, I would be enthusiastic to work on the cases of death row prisoners or Guantanamo Bay detainees. This would be a fantastic chance to gain first-hand experience of human rights law, in which I hope to practice. UT also has a human rights law centre and an Animal Legal Defence society, both of which I would be eager to participate in.

Austin is a progressive, cosmopolitan city, creative in nature, providing a vast array of social and activist opportunities. I would delight in living in the ‘live music capital of the world’ and as a vegan I would be at home in one of the most vegan-friendly cities in America. UT boasts many martial arts clubs, gyms and a dedicated practice room in which I can continue to train.

Austin, both as a university and a city, is perfectly suited to my career aspirations, personality and interests, and I would wholeheartedly seize this great opportunity.
