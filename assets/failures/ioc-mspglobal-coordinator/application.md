# Application for MSPglobal Pacific Support Regional Coordinator ([IOC-UNESCO](https://www.ioc.unesco.org))

MSPglobal Consultant to support Regional Coordination Activities and the pilot project of the Southeast Pacific

To whom it may concern,

I am writing to apply for the position of MSPglobal Consultant to support Regional Coordination Activities and the pilot project of the Southeast Pacific.

In my current role as a Senior Researcher in International Marine Policy at the Institute for Sustainable Development and International Relations (IDDRI), I am engaged in a large international project focussing on strengthening regional ocean governance, for which the Southeast Pacific is a target region. As such, I have extensively reviewed the policies of these countries, drafted a detailed report on legal and institutional frameworks in the region, organised dialogue workshops in collaboration with our partners and the Comisión Permanente del Pacífico Sur (CPPS), and developed strong relationships with key actors in the region. Most recently, I collaborated with UNEP-WCMC to organise a joint workshop on MSP in the region.

I also have a specific research interest in MSP. My PhD, which investigates the governance implications of new maritime industries under “blue economy” policies, includes a substantial component on spatial planning initiatives. I am regularly invited to lecture on MSP and other marine governance issues. I have followed the development of the MSPglobal project with interest and was pleased to attend the launch event. I recently lead the production of a paper on MSP beyond national jurisdiction as part of the special issue resulting from the 2nd International Conference on MSP, which I also attended.

My analytical and writing skills are evidenced in my extensive and varied publications. In addition to publishing 30 peer-reviewed papers, I have experience writing communications for different audiences, including policy papers, submissions to government and regulatory bodies, media articles and press releases. Keen to communicate ocean issues to a broader audience, I founded the Little Blue Letter, a weekly newsletter curated by young ocean leaders from around the world. This has become incredibly popular, with over 750 subscribers and a very high open rate. I am a confident public speaker and regularly speak at conferences, workshops, and invited lectures.

As a friendly and easy-going person, I quickly form effective working relationships with colleagues and collaborators. I am currently working on two international policy projects for which I coordinate regularly with external funders and partners, including government agencies, research institutes and UN bodies. I have previously been responsible for the coordination of a large stakeholder network and the management of consultants. At the same time, I regularly undertake solo projects on my own initiative, write papers, and produce reports with limited oversight.

I am organized and efficient in my time management, using innovative tools to automate and simplify organizational tasks. Owing to a longstanding interest in computing, I am an advanced user of all office programs, database management, writing and citation management tools, web design software and programming languages, and social media platforms. I have a foundational knowledge of GIS software. I am a fast learner, particularly with regards to computer skills, and would train myself intensively in preparation for this role.

English is my native language. I have an advanced level in French, having lived in Paris for 6 years. I have an advanced level of Spanish but am currently out of practice. I can quickly regain this level when needed - for example, I recently gave a workshop presentation in Spanish.

## OVERVIEW OF THE FUNCTIONS OF THE POST

Under the overall authority of the Director-General and under the supervision of the Assistant Director-General for the Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission (ADG/IOC), the incumbent will assist with the implementation of technical activities at the IOC Marine Policy and Regional Coordination Section (IOC/MPR) within the context of the Project MSPglobal co-financed by the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund of the European Union. The incumbent will specifically:
   Support the Project Coordinator in the day-to-day implementation of the MSPglobal work plan.    Support the coordination of the pilot project in the Southeast Pacific, in line with the description of the action by maintaining the project’s overall integrity and coherence.
   Contribute to develop the pilot case studies and to assist the Project Coordinator in responding to change and challenges in coastal and marine environments through further development of science-based management tools.
   Assist in the coordination of data and information compilation within the pilot project and submission of inputs to other activities of the project.
   Assist the development and maintenance of the MSPglobal information platform and tools.    Contribute to enhancing the collaboration with relevant public and private stakeholders in the context of the pilot project.
   Contribute to the development of multilingual web-based multimedia products and training materials.    Contribute to events, trainings, meeting reports and scientific papers and the regular reporting of activities to the Head of the MPR Section and MSPglobal Project Coordinator.
   Assist the Project Coordinator with the production of project outreach, translations and marketing materials in the languages used in the pilot project.
   Assist the Project Coordinator in any other related activity that may be assigned by the Management Team.    Assist the Head of Section on Marine Policy and Regional Coordination in activities related to the implementation of the IOC-UNESCO and the European Commission Joint Roadmap to accelerate Marine/Maritime Spatial Planning worldwide.
