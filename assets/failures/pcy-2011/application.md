What are the most important social issues the world is facing today?

(Please give detailed reasons)

I
think the most important social issue we face today is climate change.
The potential ramifications are

immense, extending to food and water security, energy, biodiversity loss and health impacts.
Furthermore,

these problems threaten global peace, as conflict is likely to erupt over resource shortages.
Many of the

negative effects of climate change are already being observed and the need to combat them is a pressing one.
I

also think that the world faces a significant challenge in developing democratic governance in all nations.

Recent events in the
Arab world and in
Nigeria and
Cote d’Ivoire show how non-‐existent or unstable

democracies can threaten peace and prosperity.
Ensuring that these recent events result in improved

democratic governance should be a priority for the international community at this time.

What is your idea of an ideal world?

To me, an ideal world would be one where the basic necessities of life are available to all human beings,

regardless of where they happen to have been born, or what gender or race they belong to.
Healthcare,

education and other social services would be easily accessible, and an entrepreneurial spirit and

attitude would push us to achieve our full potential.

life with nature, so that we would be aware of the finite nature of our planet and develop ways of living that

can sustain us into the future.
