To whom it may concern,

I am writing to apply for the position of Associate at the Federal Magistrates Court.

These experiences have familiarised me with the judiciary and the courts, and have convinced me that I would enjoy, and excel, working as an associate.

I have also volunteered in a research capacity, as well as participated in mooting competitions and model United Nations.

I feel that my experience, research skills and strong communication skills, together with my enthusiasm and diligence, make me an ideal candidate for this role.

I look forward to hearing from you.

### Research Skills

FEDERAL MAGISTRATES COURT

JOB DESCRIPTION

Associate APS5

The Associate is part of a team assisting a Federal Magistrate in the day to day management of Chambers and Court. The role of the Associate is to manage chambers and to supervise the Deputy Associate. The Federal Magistrate in whose chambers this position is located exercises the Court's general federal law jurisdiction focussing on administrative law, industrial law, trade practices, bankruptcy and human rights matters. This position will not involve exposure to family law matters.

Key responsibilities and duties:

Day-to-day running of chambers including responding to enquiries.
Supervise the work of the deputy associate.
Assist the Federal Magistrate to manage the docket by preparing and managing matters waiting for hearing. Ensure the court’s case management system is up-to-date and assist the Federal Magistrate in the preparation of orders and typing of judgments. Maintain court related relationships with various external stakeholders.
Contribute to organisational forums working for better working practices.
Undertake legal research as required by the Federal Magistrate.
Other duties as required.
