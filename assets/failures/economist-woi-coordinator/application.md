As an ambitious researcher and science communicator with a passion for ocean sustainability, I am excited to apply for the Community and Content Coordinator role with The Economist Group’s Sustainability & World Ocean Initiative. At the Institute for Sustainable Development and International Relations (IDDRI) , I produce policy-relevant research and collaborate with governments, NGOs and scientists to advocate for effective conservation and management of marine biodiversity.

I have followed the development of the World Ocean Initiative with great interest since its inception and I am keen to contribute my diverse skill set, extensive network, and comprehensive knowledge of ocean sustainability issues to help expand the Initiative and maximise its impact.

### Knowledge of ocean sustainability

Owing to a decade of experience working on ocean issues, I have extensive knowledge of marine science, law and policy, as well as a detailed understanding of the underlying geopolitical and socioeconomic context. I have published on diverse topics, including international negotiations and policy processes, blue economy, marine planning, plastic litter, tourism, ﬁsheries management, offshore oil and gas, and marine renewable energy. I recently deepened my knowledge through strategic research on emerging topics of interest, including marine geopolitics, nature-based solutions, aquaculture, and the role of China in international marine policy.

I am frequently solicited to provide advice and input to a range of processes and publications. For example, I was recently contracted by UNESCO to support expert workshops concerning the protection of marine World Heritage and draft a report presented to the World Heritage Committee, and by REN21 to cover wave and tidal energy technologies in the annual Global Status of Renewables report. I teach classes on marine policy at Sciences Po and am regularly invited to give guest lectures at the International Ocean Institute and other institutions.

### Research and writing

In addition to publishing over 30 peer-reviewed papers on a diverse range of topics, I have wide experience writing blogs, press releases and working papers. I founded and edit the Little Blue Letter, a light-hearted ocean newsletter curated by young scientists and researchers. The newsletter has over 1,000 enthusiastic subscribers, with an industry-beating open rate of around 50%.

I also have experience in author management and writing for high-level contributors. For example, I have been responsible for coordinating a global network of renewable energy experts and have solicited and edited forewords from the Chair of the UN high-seas negotiations and France’s Special Ambassador for Climate Change.

### Audience-building and community engagement

As a keen user of social media and other digital tools, I have built an extended professional network through which I am able to promote and disseminate our work. I distributed a ﬂagship publication to 10,000 followers on social media and to stakeholders and practitioners worldwide through mailing lists, personal contacts, and an op-ed in The Conversation. As a result, I was invited to write a comment piece for Nature (attached) and was interviewed by various news outlets.

I regularly organise and promote online events. For example, I recently organised a webinar on mesopelagic ﬁsheries, coordinating the release of two reports from different organisations and connecting leading scientists and policy experts to decision makers and other stakeholders. The event was well received, with 250 attendees engaging in a lively discussion with panelists. Owing to the success of the event, I am currently in discussions with a leading foundation to develop this into a more comprehensive research and communications project.
