# Application for Program Associate, Sustainable Ocean Initiative ([World Resources Institute](https://www.wri.org))

Program Associate - Sustainable Ocean Initiative | December 2020 | 1 of 2

To whom it may concern,

As an ambitious researcher and passionate advocate with extensive experience in marine policy, I am excited to apply for the Program Associate position at WRI’s Sustainable Ocean Initiative. As a Senior Researcher at the Institute for Sustainable Development and International Relations (IDDRI) , I am engaged in a range of activities that aim to strengthen ocean governance. I produce policy-relevant research and collaborate with governments, NGOs and scientists to advocate for effective conservation and management of marine biodiversity.

I have broad experience working with government ofﬁcials and other actors to promote ocean sustainability and have developed a strong awareness of the geopolitical and economic factors that inﬂuence policy. Through my diverse portfolio of research and publications, I have developed extensive knowledge of marine policy issues and the underlying science. I recently deepened my knowledge through strategic research on marine geopolitics, nature-based solutions, aquaculture, and the role of China in international marine policy.

I have followed the Ocean Panel with great interest and believe the recent call to action can encourage further commitments to sustainable management. I am keen to contribute my skills and experience to advance this agenda and maximise the impact of WRI’s work ahead of the 2021 UN Ocean Conference.

### Strategic and analytical skills

Working for a small institute, I am required to strategically develop a work programme that can generate impact and visibility with limited resources. I monitor current developments, seeking niches where we can contribute useful analysis and identifying speciﬁc roles we can fulﬁll. I have developed a varied body of research and events that has secured our position as both an objective and respected voice in international policy processes and a leader on previously neglected issues.

For example, I analysed the trafﬁc on IDDRI’s website and noted continued interest in an outdated overview of high seas issues. As UN discussions were gaining momentum, I saw an opportunity to produce a comprehensive guide to serve as an introduction for newcomers and a reference for seasoned delegates. I developed high-quality analysis, seeking feedback from reviewers with diverse perspectives, and procured forewords from the Chair of the negotiations and France’s Special Ambassador for Climate Change. I planned a communications campaign, leveraging my extensive network and social media and outreach skills to ensure wide distribution.

The report is now widely recognised as the essential guide to the negotiations and remains one of our most popular publications. It generates regular invitations from editors, journalists and event organisers, including the opportunity to write a comment piece in Nature .

### Interpersonal and political skills

I played a central role in the conception of the Partnership for Regional Ocean Governance and subsequently worked closely with colleagues to identify strategic partners and develop projects that have attracted signiﬁcant long-term support. We worked with the European Commission to secure funding and announce these projects as EU commitments to the Our Ocean Conference. I coordinate with a wide range of actors to implement these projects, including government, research institutes, and UN and EU bodies. In previous roles I have been responsible for coordinating a global community of renewable energy experts and for hiring and managing consultants.

As part of the STRONG High Seas project , I work closely with colleagues and partners to strengthen participation in international negotiations and facilitate regional exchange and collaboration between decision makers and stakeholders. At the ﬁrst UN Ocean Conference, I developed a full-day programme on regional governance, hosted by the government of Germany. This included high-level speakers and culminated in the launch of a ﬂagship report that I lead-authored, Partnering for a Sustainable Ocean .
I am open and easy-going, and ﬁnd that my genuine interest in people enables me to quickly form personal connections. These relationships have provided me and my institute with excellent opportunities, including invitations to speak at high-level closed-door workshops, lead development of signiﬁcant publications, and provide input to policy-making processes.

### Communications and events

I am a conﬁdent public speaker, representing IDDRI in various forums and regularly speaking at a range of events. I have conducted media interviews and written articles, in both English and French, and have experience communicating with diverse audiences, from scientists to the general public. I have cultivated a strong professional network and social media following that allows me to exchange with leading researchers and experts, as well as promote our work and outputs.

In addition to organising side events and conference sessions, I regularly organise and facilitate high-level expert meetings, workshops, trainings, and stakeholder dialogues. I played a key role in the conception and delivery of the Marine Regions Forum , an international invite-only conference. Adapting to current circumstances, I have quickly learned to deliver engaging online events. For example, I recently organised a webinar on mesopelagic ﬁsheries, coordinating the release of two reports from different organisations and convening leading scientists and governance experts. The event was well received, with 250 attendees engaging in a lively discussion with panelists.

Program Overview

The World Resources Institute’s (WRI) Sustainable Ocean Initiative
(SOI) is focused on accelerating progress toward a sustainable ocean economy and achievement of SDG 14 - the Ocean Goal. The Initiative supports eﬀorts to address the numerous challenges the ocean faces and encourage conservation of the marine environment alongside the sustainable use of the marine resources that are so crucial to many countries’ economic development and human well-being. Despite the central importance of a healthy ocean to the achievement of the 2030
Agenda for Sustainable Development, solutions oﬀered by the ocean, and the ocean economy, to support sustainable and equitable recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic and to address some of the world’s most pressing challenges such as food insecurity and climate mitigation and adaption have been largely overlooked.

Through the Ocean Initiative, WRI has set out to change this narrative, to build a greater understanding among governments, business leaders, ﬁnanciers and practitioners of the opportunities, solutions, values and contribution of the ocean to the global economy, human health and wellbeing, and to accelerate knowledge-based decision making and solutions that drive inclusive ocean based economic opportunities while sustaining ocean ecosystems and coastal populations. WRI serves as the Secretariat to two globally inﬂuential ocean initiatives, the High Level Panel for a Sustainable Ocean
Economy (Ocean Panel) and the Friends of Ocean Action (with the
World Economic Forum), which allows us to place cutting edge research and analysis in the hands of decision makers to shape agendas and policies at the highest levels of business and government. With ocean partners, WRI works to raise the proﬁle of the ocean on the international stage and catalyze and accelerate responsive action and innovative solutions for a sustainable ocean

economy.

World Resources Institute (WRI) is an independent, nonprofit global research organization that turns big ideas into action at the nexus of environment, economic opportunity and human well-being. We are working to address seven critical challenges that the world must overcome this decade in order to secure a sustainable future for people and the planet: climate change, energy, food, forests, water, sustainable cities, and the ocean.
We are passionate. We value our diversity of interests, skills and backgrounds. We have a flexible work environment. And we share a common goal to catalyze change that will improve the lives of people.
Our shared ideals are at the core of our approach. They include: integrity, innovation, urgency, independence and respect.
The foundation of our work is delivering high-quality research, data, maps and analysis to solve the world’s greatest environment and international development challenges, and improve people’s lives. We work with leaders in government, business and civil society to drive ambitious action and create change on the ground. Equally important, we bring together partners to develop breakthrough ideas and scale-up solutions for far-reaching, enduring impact.
We have been growing rapidly: our staff has doubled in size over the past 5 years and our operating budget is now $150 million. Founded in
1982, WRI has a global staff of 1,000+ people with work spanning 60 countries. We have offices in Africa, Brazil, China, Europe, India, Indonesia, Mexico and the United
States as well as a growing presence in other countries and regions.
WRI is committed to advancing gender and social equity for human well- being in our mission and applies this principle to our organizational and programmatic practices.
