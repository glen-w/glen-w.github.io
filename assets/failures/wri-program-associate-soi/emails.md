Many of the world's maritime powers are conspicuously absent from the
Panel, e.g. France, the USA and Russia. This is not by accident: “Negotiation
with that category of country isn’t all that easy... We decided to get a
group where high politics wouldn’t get in the way and we could be focused
on the task” - Vidar Helgesen, the former Norwegian Minister of Climate and
Environment who has been a driving force behind the initiative.
By forming a "coalition of the willing", the Panel is side-stepping the
incrementalism of global consensus-building and leading by example. Given
that these countries manage 40% of the world's coastline, their proactive
and public commitment to ocean sustainability has the potential to generate
real momentum.

While the Panel does not shy away from calling for strong conservation
action, they also stress need to sustainably manage all marine spaces and
highlight the human dimension of ocean sustainability: increasing protein
supply
