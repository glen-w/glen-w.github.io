### Activity

Attendance at the Rio+20 Conference on Sustainable Development as part of ANU’s student delegation

The Rio+20 Conference is set to be a seminal international gathering that will define the future of sustainable development and I have been selected to be part of the ANU’s student delegation.

Being part of this delegation involves writing a paper on my chosen theme and presenting it at a symposium, attending briefings before and during the conference regarding the Rio+20 process, and being actively involved in discussion and lobbying during the conference. The delegation will be meeting with other student delegations and I will also attend side-events relating to my area of interest - energy. In addition to the above activities, it is my intention to actively blog about the event as it progresses, and further develop my research paper into a full length paper for publication in a peer-reviewed journal.

I am a PhD student working on renewable energy law and policy and I have a longstanding interest in sustainable development, particularly regarding energy issues. I have previously published papers regarding development and renewable energy, and hope to develop a career path that melds these two interests.

Attendance at this conference as part of the ANU’s student delegation is a unique opportunity to observe international policymaking firsthand, while making valuable contacts and networks that will help me in my future research and career development. Having previously attended a Model United Nations Conference, and having researched such international conferences, I am excited to have the opportunity to be personally involved in an important UN conference.
