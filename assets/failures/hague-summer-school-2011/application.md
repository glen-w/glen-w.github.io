Dear ██████,

I am writing to apply for a scholarship to attend The Hague Academy of International Law.

The Academy is internationally recognised as being the premier institution for further learning in international law. Having a longstanding interest in international law, I am excited by the prospect of participating in an intensive course of study at the Peace Palace, surrounded by the world’s best international law scholars and students. To join the alumni of the Academy would be an honour.

In addition to receiving teaching from distinguished scholars, I hope to benefit from the wide range of perspectives and ideas that arise from having people of many different backgrounds in attendance. Not only do I feel that this fosters a unique educational experience, but also a social and cultural one.

The program this year particularly appeals to my interests in human rights and international courts and tribunals, and I would seek to gain a deeper understanding of the issues surrounding these topics.

If selected to receive a scholarship to attend the summer school, I would wholeheartedly immerse myself in the program, both contributing and listening actively to the contributions of others.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Age limit: under 30 years as of January 1st 2011
In general, study scholarships are in the amount of 1150 euros (from which the registration fee of 400 euros will be deducted) Travelling expenses are not normally reimbursable
Applications for scholarships must relate to only one of the two periods
Students who wish to apply for a scholarship must imperatively provide the following documents : the scholarship application form typewritten, with an identity photograph a curriculum vitae a copy of all diplomas in law, translated into one of the two languages of the Academy (French or English) indication of the number of hours of international law courses attended, together with the results and marks obtained a short letter giving reasons for the application (typewritten, 200 words) a recommendation from a █████ of international law, contained in a sealed envelope signed on the reverse side THESE SHOULD BE SENT BY REGULAR MAIL ONLY (NON-REGISTERED), TO:███████████ General of the Hague Academy of International Law9, Cité Trévise75009 Paris France
