Basics

Summary Independent researcher and consultant with 15 years of experience delivering policy-relevant research and strategic advice on ocean governance and energy policy. I combine qualitative and quantitative analysis, data visualisation and clear communication to translate complex evidence into practical insights for policymakers, international organisations, think tanks and civil-society partners. My experience includes leading major international research programmes, producing flagship knowledge products, coordinating multidisciplinary contributor networks and advising intergovernmental processes.
